# Nora: model status

Latest revision: v4 (`review-v4.png`). Mantle is a folded shell with a wavy hem and gold brooch. Hood shell and dark glass eyes with gold rims are in.
Gaps still open: the hood reads as bare head from the front, the mantle is stiff, the limbs are thin, and the body lacks the sheet's neck and chest detail. The cape still needs a real sculpt.

**First-pass draft. Not approved. Not for rigging yet.**

Built procedurally in Blender 4.0 by `build_nora.py`, from proportions in the approved design sheet.

Working:
- Head, large glass eyes with iris and pupil, halo ring, neck, waist ring, graphite joints, backpack, arms, legs, feet.
- Scale: about 0.29 m tall including the halo.
- Exports `nora.glb`.

Known gaps against the approved sheet (review-v2.png):
- Cape is a flat, stiff tent. It needs a sculpted drape with folds and a shape that wraps the shoulders.
- Limbs are thin sticks. The sheet shows fuller, mechanical limbs with visible joints.
- Feet are plain blobs.
- Colours are not yet matched to the Ivory / Sand / Champagne / Graphite / Dusty Blue palette in the sheet.

Next: an artist sculpt pass, or a revised script, followed by sign-off against the turnaround and expression sheets. Separate turnaround and expression files are still needed.
